Progress and price

Since August 2023, frontier AI capability is up 261× (METR task length, measured), OpenAI's revenue 49×, Nvidia's stock 4.4×, chip stocks 3.2× and the S&P 500 1.8×.

Is Nvidia the new Cisco? Its stock is rising faster than Cisco's did, but its valuation already hit Cisco's peak (38× sales) in 2023; at 18× today, revenue has caught up

Nvidia is up 13× since ChatGPT; at the same point after Netscape's IPO, Cisco was up 8.8× and went on to 21× before falling 84%. Cisco's price-to-sales peaked at 38×; Nvidia's reached 40× and is 18× today.

Warning signs

  • 10-year yield shock (serious): 5.05% (+106 bp vs. a year ago)
  • Yield curve (10y − 3m) (serious): Un-inverted to +87 bp after an inversion in the last 18 months (historically the dangerous phase)
  • Semiconductor (SOX) drawdown from 52-week high (serious): -20.2% from high
  • AI credit repricing (critical): CoreWeave 5y CDS hit a record ~1,190bp (Oct 15), pricing over 60% odds of default; its 2031 bonds yield ~15%. Oracle CDS touched 300bp (295 on Oct 19), trading like BB/B credit. Goldman: AI high-yield spreads >350bp and data-center debt >400bp vs ~250bp for non-AI BB/B. Amazon, Alphabet and Meta CDS at record wides.
  • Hyperscaler bond demand (warning): No jumbo hyperscaler deal since mid-September (earnings blackout ending). Goldman warns AI bond supply could outrun demand; new-issue concessions ~12bp vs ~2bp in 2025. Issuance ~$223B YTD (UBS, Sep 7).
  • AI revenue quality (warning): OpenAI run-rate reports revised down ~$20B on definitions; Big-3 net income inflated by mark-to-market gains on AI-lab stakes (Amazon $53B, Alphabet $98B).
  • Power / grid bottleneck (warning): PJM called emergency demand response Oct 13-14 (within ~900MW of its reserve margin); FERC rejected PJM's data-center curtailment plan (Oct 9); a Microsoft-linked UK data center may slip to the 2030s over grid delays.
  • Fed tightening (serious): Rate 3.75-4.00% after the Sep 16 hike. Markets price ~15% odds of another hike on Oct 28 but ~89% by December; 10y ~5.1%. Warsh: AI's path 'likely to be a very bumpy one'.
  • Official-sector warnings (warning): IMF GFSR (Oct 13) names an AI-driven correction as a near-term trigger; Georgieva (Oct 16): the world economy is 'dangerously reliant' on AI for growth. IMF adverse case: AI investment bust cuts US GDP 1.8% by 2028.
  • Crowding & sentiment (warning): BofA Oct FMS: long semiconductors is the most crowded trade; AI bubble is the #2 tail risk; net 37% overweight tech (most since Aug 2020). AAII is 48% bearish - not euphoric.
